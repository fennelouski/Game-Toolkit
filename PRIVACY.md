# Game Toolkit privacy policy

Updated September 27, 2026. Game Toolkit is developed by Nathan Fennel.

## Your game data

Player names, scores, game-night groups, dice bags, avatar images and preferences are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, supported saved data syncs through Apple's CloudKit in your private iCloud account. Nathan Fennel does not receive or have access to this private game data. You can edit or delete players, groups and dice bags in the app, and manage iCloud data through your Apple Account settings.

## Optional photos and permissions

You can choose a photo with Apple's photo picker or allow the camera to take a player avatar. The app does not upload these images to a developer-operated server. Avatar images may sync with your other game data through iCloud. Camera access is optional and can be changed in system Settings. Image Playground, where available, is provided by Apple.

Timers can use local notifications or system alarms if you enable them and grant permission. Live Activities may show timer information on your Lock Screen. These features do not send timer or player information to Nathan Fennel. You can disable them in the app or system Settings.

## Theme downloads

The app includes offline themes and may request an updated public theme catalog from game-toolkit-themes.vercel.app. Theme searches run on your device; search text, names, scores and photos are not included in catalog requests. The hosting provider receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, when serving these requests and may retain operational or security logs under its policies. The app uses no advertising identifiers, advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## External links and support

Privacy and support links open your browser. GitHub hosts this policy, and the support website handles information you choose to send through its contact form. Those services have their own privacy policies. Please avoid including private game data in a support request unless it is needed to explain your issue.

For privacy questions or deletion requests about information you have sent directly to the developer, contact [Nathan Fennel](https://nathanfennel.com/contact).
